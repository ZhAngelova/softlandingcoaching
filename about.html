There might be many principles to external success, but there is only one that leads to internal success, and that is to increase our level of consciousness.

In an ever-evolving world, the role of coaching is shifting beyond mere goal setting and performance enhancement. Soft Landing Coaching invites us to delve deeper into the essence of personal and professional growth, but from a spiritual perspective, considering not only our actions but also the higher awareness that guides them. By integrating mindfulness, emotional intelligence, and a profound sense of higher awareness, this approach fosters more meaningful connections, informed decisions, and a holistic sense of fulfilment.

It challenges us to coach not just to attain results, but for resonance, aligning inner values with outer actions in a way that transforms lives with purpose and intention.

About

Soft Landing Coaching is a gentle bridge from isolation to reconnection. A journey of growth, safety, and transformation. Whether you’re navigating a life transition in your personal life, relationships, or work, and feeling overwhelmed, isolated, disconnected, or noticing your identity slipping, this approach meets you where you are, helping you move forward.

Soft Landing Coaching offers a calm, guided arrival into a better emotional space. Through spiritual awareness, compassionate listening, personalised guidance, and practical tools, it supports your shift into your desired space, not by forcing change, but by gently inviting it. It’s about creating a safe space to land emotionally, reflect, gain clarity and reconnect with yourself and others. No matter what you’ve been through, you are not defined by your past. Change can be painless, and it is possible.

The Mission

Our mission is to support individuals from isolation to self-empowerment, helping them adjust to change, reconnect with their inner truth, and build a meaningful, growth-filled life in their everyday reality.

My Story

I' Hello! I'm Coach Rah, and I'm here to create a space where you can grow, reflect, and connect with others. I'm passionate about human behaviour and helping people fulfil their potential. Growing up, I was told I couldn’t pursue my dreams, I was often bullied and grew up in an abusive environment, and as a result, I developed negative beliefs that shaped my life. It wasn’t until my 40s that I transformed my life. Through that journey, I learned I was not defined by my past. Most importantly, I discovered that my passion for helping others stay focused on what they truly want to achieve is the greatest gift I can offer. I've been where you are. Our stories may be different but many of the struggles we face are the same. I never had the support I needed and as a result I spent decades struggling with depression on my own. That experience is why I deeply believe in the power of conversation, connection, and community when navigating life's challenges and changes. By engaging with one another and hearing our experiences we gain new perspectives and deeper insights. These moments of connection are essential for developing self-awareness, building resilience, and creating meaningful growth. I will be here to support, encourage, and cheer you on every step of the way not only because I care deeply about mental wellbeing but because I understand how easy it is to lose sight of your potential. I've seen far too many people lose hope before realising what they are truly capable of. I believe that when we come together, share our stories, and support one another anything becomes possible. Your journey forward can begin today.
